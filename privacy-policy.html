YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy Policy

Your trust is important to us. Here's how Liwi-Ka collects, uses, and protects your information.

01

Introduction

At Liwi-Ka, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when you use our website, place an order, or contact our Customer Care team.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices you may have regarding your information.

02

Information We Collect

When you use Liwi-Ka, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery and billing address
  • Order and purchase details
  • Payment-related information required to process your order
  • Information you provide when contacting Customer Care
  • Technical information such as browser and device information

03

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Confirm purchases and payments
  • Provide Customer Care and support
  • Respond to questions and complaints
  • Send important order-related communications
  • Improve our website, products, and services
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized activity
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal requirements

04

Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers.

Liwi-Ka does not intend to store complete debit-card, credit-card, or banking credentials on its own servers.

Payment information is handled according to the security practices and policies of the payment provider used for the transaction.

05

Sharing of Information

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business, including:

  • Payment service providers
  • Delivery and logistics partners
  • Website and technology service providers
  • Customer-support service providers

We do not sell your personal information for money.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or by a lawful government or regulatory authority.

06

Cookies

Liwi-Ka may use cookies and similar technologies to help the website function properly, remember preferences, understand website usage, and improve your experience.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

07

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the details provided below.

09

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

11

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

12

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact our Customer Care team.
