YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy Policy

Your trust is important to us. Here's how Liwi-Ka collects, uses, and protects your information.

01

Introduction

At Liwi-Ka, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when you use our website, place an order, or contact our Customer Care team.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices you may have regarding your information.

02

Information We Collect

When you use Liwi-Ka, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery and billing address
  • Order and purchase details
  • Payment-related information required to process your order
  • Information you provide when contacting Customer Care
  • Technical information such as browser and device information

03

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Confirm purchases and payments
  • Provide Customer Care and support
  • Respond to questions and complaints
  • Send important order-related communications
  • Improve our website, products, and services
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized activity
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal requirements

04

Payment Information

Online payments are made by UPI and are processed by Cashfree Payments, a payment gateway. You enter your UPI details only in your UPI app or Cashfree’s secure payment screen. Liwi-Ka never sees or stores your UPI PIN, card or bank-login details.

We receive only the result of the payment (paid / failed), the amount and a payment reference so we can confirm and, if needed, refund your order.

Cash on Delivery orders involve no online payment data.

05

Sharing of Information

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business, including:

  • Payment service providers
  • Delivery and logistics partners
  • Website and technology service providers
  • Customer-support service providers

We do not sell your personal information for money.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or by a lawful government or regulatory authority.

06

Cookies

Liwi-Ka may use cookies and similar technologies to help the website function properly, remember preferences, understand website usage, and improve your experience.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

07

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the details provided below.

09

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

10

Account sign-in, email and delivery OTPs

We use your phone number (verified by a one-time SMS code) and/or your Google account to sign you in, and your email address (verified by a one-time email code) to send order confirmations, your delivery OTP and receipts.

One-time codes are stored only in scrambled (hashed or encrypted) form, expire quickly, and can be tried only a limited number of times. Delivery OTPs are visible only to you and are verified by our team at the time of delivery.

11

Delivery location and map data

When you place an order we collect your delivery address, PIN code and the map pin / coordinates you choose, so our team and delivery partner can find you. If you tap “use my location”, your device asks your permission first. Address search uses OpenStreetMap / Nominatim, and our team opens your pin in Google Maps to navigate.

12

Who processes your data

We use the following service providers (“data processors”) only to run the shop:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, hosting, cloud functions — servers may be outside India)
  • Cashfree Payments (UPI payment processing)
  • Email delivery provider (to send OTP and order emails)
  • OpenStreetMap / Google Maps (address search and navigation)
  • Google sign-in and reCAPTCHA (to prevent abuse)

We do not sell your personal data.

13

How long we keep data

Order and payment records are kept as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal requirements (generally up to 8 years). Support messages are kept while needed to resolve your query. One-time codes and rate-limit records are deleted automatically within hours or days. You can ask us to delete your account data, subject to records we must keep by law.

14

Your rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

You have the right to access a summary of your personal data, correct or update it, ask for its erasure, withdraw consent, nominate another person to exercise your rights if you cannot, and complain about how your data is handled. Write to the Grievance Officer below; we will respond within the time required by law. If you are unsatisfied you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
