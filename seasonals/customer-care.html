THE LITTLE DETAILS

Frequently asked questions.

01 How long does delivery take? +

Delivery timelines and estimated dates are shown during checkout. If you already placed an order, contact us with your order number and we'll help you check its status.

02 Can I modify or cancel my order? +

Please contact customer care as soon as possible. We'll check the order status and let you know what changes can still be made.

03 Do you offer gift packaging? +

Selected Liwi-Ka chocolates can be prepared as gifts. If you have a specific gifting request, mention it in your message and we'll guide you.

04 Where can I find ingredients? +

If you need ingredient information for a particular chocolate, tell us the product name and we'll help you with the details.

05 What if my order arrives damaged? +

Contact us with your order details and a description of the issue. We'll help you with the next steps.

06 Still have a question? +

That's completely fine. Use the contact form above and send us your question directly.
