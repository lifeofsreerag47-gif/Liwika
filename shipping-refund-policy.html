Shipping & Refund Policy

Clear, fair terms for how your chocolates reach you — and what happens if something goes wrong.

01

Where and how we deliver

We currently deliver only within Visakhapatnam (PIN codes beginning 530). Please drop an exact map pin at checkout — our delivery partner uses it to find you.

Delivery fee is ₹20 per order, and free for orders of ₹150 or more (the amount is shown before you pay). Estimated delivery times are shown at checkout and are estimates, not guarantees.

02

Delivery OTP — your protection

Every order has a 6-digit delivery OTP. We email it to you and show it in My Orders. Share it with our delivery partner only when the parcel is in your hands. The order is marked delivered only after the OTP is verified.

Never share the OTP on a call, chat or message, or before you receive the parcel. If you think someone has seen it, tap New OTP in My Orders to cancel the old one and get a fresh code.

03

Payment methods

We accept UPI (processed securely by Cashfree Payments) and Cash on Delivery (COD). We do not accept cards or net-banking.

COD is available for orders up to ₹5,000. Please keep the exact amount ready; payment is recorded when the delivery OTP is verified.

An unpaid UPI order is held for 30 minutes; after that it is released automatically and the stock is returned. No money is taken for an order that is not completed.

04

Cancellation

You can cancel an unpaid order yourself from My Orders at any time.

For a paid order, please contact us as soon as possible. Because our chocolates are freshly prepared, we can cancel only before the order moves to “Preparing”. Once preparation or dispatch has started, a cancellation may not be possible.

We may cancel an order if an item is unavailable, the address is outside our delivery area, or we suspect fraud. In that case any amount paid is refunded in full.

05

Returns and replacements

Chocolates and food items are perishable and cannot be returned once delivered. This does not affect your statutory rights.

If your order arrives damaged, melted, incorrect or incomplete, contact us on the day of delivery (within 24 hours) with your order number and a clear photo. After checking, we will offer a replacement or a refund.

06

Refunds

Approved refunds for UPI payments are returned to the same UPI account / bank account used to pay, normally within 5–7 working days of approval, depending on your bank.

For COD orders, no refund is needed unless you paid and the order is later cancelled; any such amount is returned by bank transfer or UPI after we verify the details.

If you were charged but see no order, do not pay again — it is confirmed automatically, or refunded if it cannot be fulfilled. Write to us with the payment reference.

07

Failed delivery

If we cannot reach you or no one can receive the parcel, we will try to contact you. Because the product is perishable, an undelivered order may be cancelled; where it is cancelled through no fault of yours, you will be refunded as described above.
